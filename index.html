Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be experienced to join?

No. The Garden is designed for curious beginner and intermediate practitioners who want to understand their craft more deeply.

Is this only an October course?

No. Divination Beyond the Veil + Mirror Magic is October’s monthly study. The Black Garden Society is an ongoing membership with new monthly subjects, community, ritual, and the growing Greenhouse reference library.

What is The Greenhouse?

The Greenhouse is Nightshade’s growing reference library of magical resources, including botanicals, correspondences, spell oils, rituals, divination material, mini-lessons, and other educational references.

Do I have to practice the same way as everyone else?

No. The community is meant for learning, discussion, questions, and exchanging perspectives. You do not have to practice exactly like Nightshade or anyone else inside the Garden.

How much is membership?

Membership is currently $25/month. New member pricing increases to $49/month in January.
